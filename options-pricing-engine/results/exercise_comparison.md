# American exercise in the surface: before and after

Generated by `python scripts/run_analysis.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Data: SPY option chain from Yahoo Finance, captured 2026-09-18 11:09 ET, spot 759.87; discounting from the same day's Treasury curve. 1734 matched call/put pairs.

Both columns are computed in the same run, from the same cleaned quotes, rate curve, thinning and calibration settings. *European parity* is `build_surface(..., exercise="european")`: quotes inverted as quoted, forward from plain put-call parity. *American-corrected* is the default: each quote's early-exercise premium (201-step Leisen-Reimer lattice, continuous dividend yield) is removed by a per-quote fixed point, and the forward is re-solved from de-Americanised parity, the two iterated together. Vol errors are model minus market, in vol points; *body* is |k| < 0.15, the *short-dated put wing* k < -0.15 and tau < 0.15 years.
